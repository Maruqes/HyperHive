# HyperHive narration

**Voice:** local Kokoro, bm_george
**Direction:** Calm, measured British English.

## Line 1 — Frame 1

    Your infrastructure should feel connected. HyperHive brings your homelab or small datacenter together, under one control plane.

## Line 2 — Frame 2

    Create virtual machines. Clone them, back them up, and move workloads between hosts. Keep compute under your control.

## Line 3 — Frame 3

    Bring storage into the same picture. Manage B T R F S, N F S shares, and disk health alongside your machines.

## Line 4 — Frame 4

    Connect services to the outside world. Manage reverse proxies, certificates, D N S, and WireGuard from the same platform.

## Line 5 — Frame 5

    And connect your AI tools through M C P. Inspect resources and perform supported operations through explicit tool contracts.

## Line 6 — Frame 6

    A central master. Host agents where the work happens. HyperHive. Your infrastructure, one control plane. Built for your own hive.
