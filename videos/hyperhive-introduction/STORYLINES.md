# Storylines
1. **One fabric** (selected): a workload travels through a physical cluster diagram, shared storage and network routes; each new connection explains why one control plane matters. Opens on “Your infrastructure. Together.” Dark slate and cyan from the frontend; a light blueprint world for storage. Calm English narration, minimal electronic music and restrained cut effects.
2. **A day in the homelab**: one operator's morning, a wall of requests resolving into a single task. Requires real operator footage and interface recordings for its strongest version.
3. **Inside the hive**: an architectural field guide, with a single continuous camera trip from the control plane down to libvirt and the hosts. More technical than a first introduction.
